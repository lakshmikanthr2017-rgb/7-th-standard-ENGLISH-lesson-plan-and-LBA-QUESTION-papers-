Sample LBA Model Assessment: Unit 1 (A Tiger in the House & Self-Reliance)

Max Marks: 100 Scale | Time: 45 Minutes

I. Answer the following questions in one or two sentences:

  1. Where was Timothy the tiger cub discovered by Grandfather in the Terai jungle?
  2. What changes came over Timothy as he grew up into a full-sized tiger?
  3. According to the poem 'Self-Reliance', what is the true source of human strength?

II. Reference to Context:

  1. "I'm giving him to the zoo, that's what I'm doing."
    a) Who said this? b) Why was Timothy being transferred to the zoo?

III. Vocabulary & Grammar:

  1. Fill in the blank with the appropriate article: Grandfather took ___ small tiger cub home.
  2. Give the antonym for: 'fierce'.

5E Pedagogical Model Structure

1. Engage (Hook & Prior Knowledge)

Show pictures of wild animals or pets. Ask students to share experiences about domesticating wild animals or visiting a zoo.

2. Explore (Interactive Reading & Discovery)

Students read aloud paragraphs 1 to 5 of 'A Tiger in the House'. Identify unfamiliar vocabulary (e.g., *Terai, snarl, retriever*).

3. Explain (Concepts & Grammar Clarification)

Teacher explains the narrative structure, character traits of Grandfather and Timothy, and reviews indefinite articles (*a/an*).

4. Elaborate (Application & Extension)

Students complete textual comprehension exercises and draft a short paragraph describing an animal encounter.

5. Evaluate (Assessment & Feedback)

Conduct short oral questioning and LBA written test items to verify achievement of learning outcomes based on the 0-100 grade scale.
